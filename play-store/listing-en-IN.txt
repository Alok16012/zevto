APP NAME
Zavtoo: RO Service & Parts

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Book RO service, shop purifiers and spare parts, and manage technician visits.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Zavtoo brings water purifier shopping and service management into one app for customers, technicians and RO dealers.

FOR CUSTOMERS
• Browse RO water purifiers and spare parts.
• Request repair, installation, filter replacement and other available services.
• Choose a service address and appointment slot.
• View your orders, service history and booking status.
• Track your assigned technician during an active ride when location sharing is available.
• Send support messages and choose photos to explain an RO issue.
• Manage saved addresses and account details.

FOR TECHNICIANS
• View assigned jobs and customer service details.
• Manage service areas and update job progress.
• Share live location during an active ride with permission.
• Manage parts, stock requests and earnings records.

FOR DEALERS
• Manage shop information, product listings and orders.
• Coordinate technicians and service work.
• Review business and settlement records.

An internet connection is required. Service availability, products and appointment slots depend on your location and the available technician or dealer network. Technician and dealer access may require Zavtoo verification.

Location is used for address selection and active technician tracking with permission. Camera and photo access are used when you choose to add a picture. You can manage these permissions in your device settings.
